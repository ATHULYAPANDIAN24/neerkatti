NEERKATTI

An AI water-keeper for Chennai's wetlands

We compared satellite images of the Pallikaranai watershed from the 2018 and 2026 dry seasons. An AI model labels every 10 m square of land. Our rule flags water and marsh that turned into buildings. 28.1 hectares are flagged as built over.

Method

How it works

Steps 1–4 run today on real data. Steps 5–6 are built in the 24-hour final round, as the rules require.

Sentinel-2 satellite

Free images of every lake, every 5 days.

WORKING NOW

Dynamic World AI

A deep-learning model labels each 10 m square: water, marsh, built, soil.

WORKING NOW

Change rule v1

2018 water/marsh that is built in 2025 and 2026. Lake edges ignored.

WORKING NOW

Live map + open data

All 36 patches on a public web map, each with a close-up and an AI confidence level, and a GeoJSON anyone can reuse.

WORKING NOW

Alert + citizen photo

Alerts for new changes, with radar that sees through monsoon clouds. A vision model tags the citizen's photo: dumping, construction, sewage or no change.

FINAL ROUND

Complaint PDF

Drafted in Tamil and English, with dated before/after proof.

FINAL ROUND
Honest numbers

How we cut false alarms

94 ha → 28 ha

Flagged loss under the first rule and under the strict rule. We report the smaller, safer number.

Every alert says "possible change, needs a ground check". NEERKATTI never names or accuses any owner.
