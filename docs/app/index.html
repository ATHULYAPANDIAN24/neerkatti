Top 10 flagged patches

    Tap a patch to zoom to it and see its 2018 | 2026 close-up. We checked all ten by eye on those close-ups and on current high-resolution satellite imagery: 9 show building, paving or filling, and 1 is unclear (notes). A visual check, not a ground survey.

    How a patch gets flagged
    1. Dynamic World labels every 10 m square of each Sentinel-2 image: water, trees, grass, marsh, crops, shrubs, buildings, bare soil.
    2. For each year we keep the most common label for each square during Feb–May.
    3. A square is flagged if it was water or marsh in 2018 (ignoring the outer ~20 m rim of each water body) and is buildings in both 2025 and 2026.
    4. Patches smaller than about 0.1 ha are dropped as noise.

    What's different
    • ISRO's wetland atlas compares snapshots ten years apart (2006–07 vs 2017–18).
    • TN-SWIP, the state's satellite pilot, sends its alerts to officials (Deccan Chronicle, 23 Aug 2026).
    • NEERKATTI is open to anyone, flags a square only if it stays built-up two years running, and hands over the data. Continuous alerts and complaints come in the final round.
    How far to trust it
    • Dynamic World's own validation gives 86.7% user's accuracy for buildings but only 36.8% producer's accuracy for marsh (Brown et al. 2022, Tables 7–8). It misses marsh, so 28.1 ha is a cautious count.
    • 10 m squares miss small dumping, and monsoon clouds limit us to dry-season images for now.
    • The top-10 check is a visual check of imagery, not a ground survey.
    Cost and data
    • ₹0 data cost: free Sentinel-2 images and Dynamic World labels, Google Earth Engine (non-commercial), hosted free on GitHub Pages. No server and no API keys.
    • Sentinel-2 photos: median of the clear Feb–May images of each year.
    • Study area: 80.17–80.26°E, 12.86–12.99°N. Made by analysis/app_assets.py.
