Top 10 flagged patches

    Tap a patch to zoom to it. We checked all ten by eye on before/after and high-resolution satellite images: 9 show construction, paving or filling, and 1 is unclear. Sites A, B and C are the three largest.

    How a patch gets flagged
    1. Dynamic World labels every 10 m square of each Sentinel-2 image: water, trees, grass, marsh, crops, shrubs, buildings, bare soil.
    2. For each year we keep the most common label for each square during Feb–May.
    3. A square is flagged if it was water or marsh in 2018 (ignoring the outer ~20 m rim of each water body) and is buildings in both 2025 and 2026.
    4. Patches smaller than about 0.1 ha are dropped as noise.
    Data and downloads
    • Sentinel-2 photos: median of the clear Feb–May images of each year.
    • Study area: 80.17–80.26°E, 12.86–12.99°N.
    • Download all patches (GeoJSON)

    Final round (24 h): alerts for new changes, a citizen photo check, and a complaint letter as PDF.
