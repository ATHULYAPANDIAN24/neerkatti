NEERKATTI - 3-MINUTE SCRIPT
Team Sternritters - DEXATHON 2026 final

WHO DOES WHAT
  DRIVER    holds the phone and taps. Says nothing.
  CLOSER    opens and closes.
  ARCHITECT explains the system and the numbers.
  IMPACT    explains the risks and the letter.
Words in [brackets] are what the Driver taps. Never read the brackets aloud.
Speak slowly. Pause after every number.

==================================================================

0:00  CLOSER  - The problem
[Overview screen]

"Pallikaranai Marsh is one of Chennai's last big wetlands.
It soaks up floodwater for south Chennai, and it is being filled in, piece by piece.

NEERKATTI is an AI water-keeper. It reads free satellite images
from every dry season since 2018 and shows where water and marsh became buildings:
28.1 hectares, in 36 patches. Two-thirds of that happened since 2023."

------------------------------------------------------------------

0:30  CLOSER  - Understanding and prioritisation

"From the mentors' feedback, we picked the two things that mattered most:
make the architecture easy to see, and make every answer clear and checkable.
We did those first. Then we checked every claim we make against our own data."

------------------------------------------------------------------

0:50  ARCHITECT  - High-priority improvement: the architecture
[About -> How it works]

"This is the whole system, on one screen.
Google Earth Engine runs Google's Dynamic World AI over free Sentinel-2 satellite images.
The AI labels every 10-metre square as water, marsh or buildings.
Our rule flags squares that were water or marsh in 2018
and are buildings in both 2025 and 2026.
The results are saved as small files, so the phone needs
no server, no database and no API key.
It costs nothing to run, and it works offline."

------------------------------------------------------------------

1:25  ARCHITECT  - Measurable quality improvement
[Year by year -> drag the slider -> tap patch 1]

"What got better, in numbers.
Every number on screen comes straight from the data file. None is typed in by hand.
Our filters turned 26 raw detections into 5 alerts worth checking.
Our own code is about 67 kilobytes compressed,
and the demo video downloads nothing until you press play."

------------------------------------------------------------------

1:50  CLOSER  - Testing and validation

"We test it three ways.
62 automated tests run on every change.
We checked the live site end to end.
And we checked by eye: of the top 10 patches, 9 are real changes and 1 is unclear.
We show the unclear one too."

------------------------------------------------------------------

2:10  IMPACT  - Weaknesses and risks
[Act -> Write the letter -> tap the Tamil option]

"Our weaknesses, and what we did about them.
The AI misses some marsh, so 28.1 hectares is a minimum, not the full loss.
10-metre squares can't see small dumping,
so every patch says 'possible change, needs a ground check'.
Our photo-check service used an AI model Google is retiring,
so we moved it to a supported model before it could break.
And the result is this: a complaint letter, in Tamil or English,
asking the right authority to inspect."

------------------------------------------------------------------

2:40  CLOSER  - Ownership and justification
[Alerts -> tap A3]

"Every choice here has a reason.
No server, so it is free and cannot crash.
A proven AI model instead of our own, because there is no ground truth to train on.
'Coming' labels instead of fake features.

And this matters now: the High Court is due to hear the case
on the marsh's protected zone on 11 November.
This alert is 700 metres from this campus.

What would you like us to show you in detail?"

[STOP TALKING. Wait for their question.]

LIKELY QUESTIONS - answer in one sentence, then stop

Which feedback did you act on?
  "A clearer architecture and clearer answers. The diagram is in the app, and every claim is checked against the data."

What was the most important change?
  "Putting the architecture on screen, because everything else is explained from it."

How do you know it improved?
  "62 tests pass on every change, every number comes from the data, and we fixed two overclaims we found ourselves."

What AI do you use?
  "Google's Dynamic World. It labels every 10-metre square in Sentinel-2 images."

How accurate is it?
  "86.7% on buildings, 36.8% on marsh. It misses marsh, so our number is a minimum."

Does the AI run on the phone?
  "No. It ran once in Earth Engine. The phone reads the labels it saved."

Why no server?
  "Everything is worked out in advance, so it is free, fast, and has nothing to hack."

What if a patch is wrong?
  "Every patch says 'possible change, needs a ground check', and the letter asks an official to inspect."

What is not built yet?
  "The photo check and the radar layer. They are labelled 'Coming', not faked."

Has anyone used it?
  "Not yet."

What would you do next?
  "Deploy the photo check, then share it with resident groups around the marsh."

Don't know an answer?
  "I don't know. Here is what I do know: ..."   Never guess a number.
