NEERKATTI - ROUND 3 SCRIPT (fits in 3 minutes)
Mentor Feedback Implementation & Improvement
Order follows the judges' slide: Understand - Implement - Improve - Test - Risks - Ownership

WHO DOES WHAT
  DRIVER    holds the phone and taps. Says nothing.
  CLOSER    opens and closes.
  ARCHITECT explains the system and the numbers.
  IMPACT    explains the risks and the letter.
[Brackets] = what the Driver taps. Never read them aloud.
Speak slowly. Pause after every number. Total speaking time: about 2 min 25 s.

==================================================================

0:00  CLOSER  -  1. UNDERSTANDING & PRIORITISATION
[Overview screen]

"NEERKATTI finds where Chennai's Pallikaranai wetland became buildings,
using free satellite images and AI.
Our mentors gave us two pieces of feedback:
make the technical architecture clear, and make every answer clear.
We fixed the architecture first, because everything else depends on it."

------------------------------------------------------------------

0:20  ARCHITECT  -  2. HIGH-PRIORITY IMPROVEMENT
[About -> How it works]

"So the architecture is now inside the app, on one screen.
Google Earth Engine runs the Dynamic World AI over free Sentinel-2 satellite images.
Our rule flags land that was water or marsh in 2018
and is buildings in both 2025 and 2026.
The results are small files on your phone:
no server, no database, no API key."

------------------------------------------------------------------

0:45  ARCHITECT  -  3. MEASURABLE QUALITY IMPROVEMENT
[Year by year -> drag the slider -> tap patch 1]

"What improved, in numbers:
every number on screen comes from the data file. None is typed in by hand.
Our filters cut 26 raw detections down to 5 alerts.
Our code is 67 kilobytes compressed, and it works offline.
The result: 28.1 hectares of wetland became buildings since 2018,
two-thirds of it since 2023."

------------------------------------------------------------------

1:10  CLOSER  -  4. TESTING & VALIDATION

"We tested every change.
62 automated tests run on every update, and all of them pass.
We checked the live site end to end.
And we checked by eye: 9 of the top 10 patches are real changes,
1 is unclear, and we show that one too."

------------------------------------------------------------------

1:30  IMPACT  -  5. WEAKNESS & RISK RESOLUTION
[Act -> Write the letter -> tap the Tamil option]

"Risks we resolved: two overclaims in our slides, corrected.
An AI model Google is retiring, replaced before it could break.
The limit we cannot remove: 10-metre pixels miss small dumping.
So every patch says 'possible change, needs a ground check',
and the app turns it into this letter, in Tamil or English,
asking an official to inspect."

------------------------------------------------------------------

1:58  CLOSER  -  6. OWNERSHIP, ADAPTABILITY & JUSTIFICATION
[Alerts -> tap A3]

"We own our decisions.
No server, so it is free and cannot crash.
A proven AI model, because there is no ground truth to train our own.
Unfinished features say 'Coming'. They are never faked.
These changes made NEERKATTI clearer, more reliable and more honest.
And it matters now: the High Court is due to hear the marsh case on 11 November.
This alert is 700 metres from this campus.
Thank you."

[STOP. Wait for their question.]
