NEERKATTI - ROUND 3 SCRIPT (fits in 3 minutes)
Mentor Feedback Implementation & Improvement
Order follows the judges' slide: Understand - Implement - Improve - Test - Risks - Ownership

WHO DOES WHAT
  DRIVER    holds the phone and taps. Says nothing.
  CLOSER    opens and closes.
  ARCHITECT explains the system and the numbers.
  IMPACT    explains the risks, the photo check and the letters.
[Brackets] = what the Driver taps. Never read them aloud.
Speak slowly. Pause after every number. Total speaking time: about 2 min 30 s.

==================================================================

0:00  CLOSER  -  1. UNDERSTANDING & PRIORITISATION
[Overview screen]

"NEERKATTI finds where Chennai's Pallikaranai wetland became buildings,
using free satellite images and AI.
Our mentors told us two things:
make the technical architecture clear, and make every answer clear.
We fixed the architecture first, because everything else depends on it."

------------------------------------------------------------------

0:20  ARCHITECT  -  2. HIGH-PRIORITY IMPROVEMENT
[About -> How it works]

"The architecture is now one screen in the app.
Google's Dynamic World AI labels free Sentinel-2 images.
Our rule flags water or marsh from 2018 that is buildings in both 2025 and 2026.
The AI ran once, through Google Earth Engine's Python API.
The website only reads the saved results.
The one live API call is the optional photo check,
through our Worker, which holds the key."

------------------------------------------------------------------

0:50  ARCHITECT  -  3. MEASURABLE QUALITY IMPROVEMENT
[Year by year -> drag the slider -> tap patch 1]

"In numbers: every number on screen now comes from the data file,
none typed by hand.
Our filters cut 26 raw detections to 5 alerts.
The result: 28.1 hectares of wetland became buildings since 2018,
two-thirds of it since 2023."

------------------------------------------------------------------

1:08  CLOSER  -  4. TESTING & VALIDATION

"62 automated tests run on every update, and all pass.
We checked the live site and the photo check end to end.
By eye, 9 of the top 10 patches are real changes and 1 is unclear,
and we show that one too."

------------------------------------------------------------------

1:25  IMPACT  -  5. WEAKNESS & RISK RESOLUTION
[Act -> point at "Report what you see" -> Write the letter -> tap the Tamil option -> tap RTI follow-up]

"Risks resolved: two overclaims in our slides, corrected.
An AI model Google is retiring, replaced before it could break.
10-metre pixels miss small dumping, so now a citizen's photo is read
by Gemini in about 3 seconds: construction, dumping, sewage, no change or unclear.
We store nothing, and it still says 'needs a ground check'.
Then this letter, in Tamil or English.
If a complaint is ignored, the app writes an RTI application,
and by law the office must answer within 30 days. Then a first appeal.
The evidence is public and dated."

------------------------------------------------------------------

2:05  CLOSER  -  6. OWNERSHIP, ADAPTABILITY & JUSTIFICATION
[Close the letter -> Alerts -> tap A3]

"We own our decisions.
The map needs no server, so it is free and cannot crash.
A proven AI model, because there is no ground truth to train our own.
Radar is not built yet, so it says 'Coming'.
And it matters now: the High Court is due to hear the marsh case on 11 November.
This alert is 700 metres from this campus.
Thank you."

[STOP. Wait for their question.]

IF THEY ASK - one sentence, then stop

What feedback did you get?
  "Make the architecture clear and make the answers clear. The diagram is now in the app."

Most important change?
  "The architecture on screen, because everything else is explained from it."

How do you know it improved?
  "62 tests pass, every number comes from the data, and we fixed two overclaims."

Which AI model?
  "Google's Dynamic World V1 for the map. The optional photo check uses Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite."

Does it use any API?
  "The AI ran once, through Google Earth Engine's Python API. The website only reads
   the saved results. The one live API call is the optional photo check, through our
   Worker, which holds the key."

Most landowners are politicians. Even if citizens complain, what can they do?
  "If a complaint is ignored, the app writes an RTI application, and by law the office
   must answer within 30 days. Then a first appeal. The evidence is public and dated."

How does the photo check work?
  "A citizen's photo, read by Gemini in about 3 seconds as construction, dumping, sewage,
   no change or unclear. We store nothing, and it still says 'needs a ground check'."

Does Google keep the photo?
  "NEERKATTI keeps nothing. On Google's free tier, Google may use it to improve its AI,
   so the phone strips GPS first, and we ask people to leave faces out."

How accurate is the AI?
  "86.7% on buildings, 36.8% on marsh. It misses marsh, so our number is a minimum."

Does the AI run on the phone?
  "No. It ran once in Earth Engine. The phone reads the labels it saved."

What is not built yet?
  "The radar layer. It says 'Coming', not faked."

Has anyone used it?
  "Not yet."

Don't know?
  "I don't know. Here is what I do know: ..."  Never guess a number.
