NEERKATTI

An AI water-keeper for Chennai's wetlands · Team Sternritters · DEXATHON 2026

86.7% user's accuracy for buildings but only 36.8% producer's accuracy for marsh

What it does

Finds where Pallikaranai's marsh and water became buildings, season by season since , from free Sentinel-2 images and Google's Dynamic World AI. Flags fresh filling as alerts, and writes a complaint letter in English or Tamil to the office that can act.

Who the letter goes to

  • Tamil Nadu State Wetland Authority
  • Greater Chennai Corporation
  • Tamil Nadu Forest Department

Why it costs ₹0

The heavy analysis runs once in Google Earth Engine and is saved as plain files. The site is HTML, CSS and JavaScript: no server, no database, no API key. It works offline after one visit.

Limits, said plainly

  • A 10 m pixel cannot see one house; small dumping is missed.
  • Every patch is a possible change that needs a ground check. NEERKATTI never names an owner.
  • The photo check and the radar layer are not built yet.
