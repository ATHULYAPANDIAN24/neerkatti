WHAT NEERKATTI IS
NEERKATTI shows where Chennai's Pallikaranai wetland has been turned into buildings.
It uses free Sentinel-2 satellite images and Google's Dynamic World AI.
Since 2018, 28.1 hectares of water and marsh have become buildings, in 36 patches.
Two-thirds of that happened since 2023.


1. UNDERSTANDING & PRIORITISATION - the feedback we received
Our mentors told us two things:
  - Make the technical architecture clear.
  - Make our answers clear and simple.
We fixed the architecture first, because the whole project depends on it.


2. HIGH-PRIORITY IMPROVEMENTS - what we implemented
  - A "How it works" diagram inside the app (About section). It shows every part
    of the system, why we chose it, and its limits.
  - Every number on screen now comes directly from the data file.
    No number is typed in by hand.
  - We checked every claim against our data and corrected two that said more
    than the data shows.


3. MEASURABLE QUALITY IMPROVEMENT
  - Hand-typed numbers on screen: 1 -> 0.
  - Overclaims in our slides: 2 -> 0.
  - Noise filters: 26 raw detections -> 5 alerts worth checking.
  - App code: about 67 KB compressed. Works offline after one visit.
  - A new phone-friendly layout that works on small screens.
  - A complaint letter generated in Tamil and English.


4. TESTING & VALIDATION
  - 62 automated tests run on every update. All pass.
  - We tested the live website end to end.
  - We checked results by eye: 9 of the top 10 patches are real changes and
    1 is unclear. We show the unclear one too.


5. WEAKNESS & RISK RESOLUTION
  - Risk: our photo-check service used an AI model Google is retiring.
    Fixed: moved to a supported model (Gemini 3.8 Flash).
  - Weakness: the AI misses some marsh. So 28.1 hectares is a minimum,
    and we say so on screen.
  - Weakness: 10-metre pixels cannot see small dumping. So every patch is marked
    "possible change, needs a ground check", and the app creates a letter
    asking an official to inspect.
  - Risk: no network at the venue. The app works offline after one visit.


6. OWNERSHIP, ADAPTABILITY & JUSTIFICATION
  - No server: free to run, nothing to crash, nothing to hack.
  - A proven AI model instead of our own: there is no ground truth to train on,
    so a tested model is more honest.
  - Unfinished features (photo check, radar) are labelled "Coming".
    We never fake them.
  - These changes made NEERKATTI clearer, more reliable and more honest.


WHY IT MATTERS NOW
The Madras High Court is due to hear the case on the marsh's protected zone
on 11 November 2026. Our nearest alert is about 700 metres from this campus.

Thank you.
